Prototype privacy design
DailyGlow currently stores profile-style preferences, favorites and cycle entries in your browser's local storage. This prototype does not send those entries to a DailyGlow server.

What we collect in this prototype

Only information you actively enter into the app and the local settings needed to remember it on this device.
